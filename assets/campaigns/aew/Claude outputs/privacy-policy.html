Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This page explains what information this website collects and how it is used. This site is a personal design portfolio belonging to Naomi Schaefer — it is not a store, it does not require an account, and it does not sell or share personal information with third parties for advertising purposes.

Hosting & server information

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub Pages may automatically log basic technical information for every visit — such as IP address, browser type, and the pages requested — for security and reliability purposes. This site owner does not have access to personally identifying details from these logs beyond what GitHub's own hosting infrastructure records. See GitHub's Privacy Statement for details.

Fonts

This site loads typefaces from Google Fonts. Loading a font this way sends a request from your browser directly to Google's servers, which may log technical information such as your IP address. See Google's Privacy Policy for details.

Cookies & similar technologies

This website does not set its own cookies and does not use tracking pixels. Your browser may still handle standard technical mechanisms (such as caching) used to load pages efficiently; these are not used here for tracking.

Analytics

This site does not currently use any analytics or visitor-tracking service. If an analytics tool is added in the future, this policy will be updated first to name the specific provider, describe what it collects, and explain any choices available to visitors.

Contact form

The Contact section of this site includes a message form. At this time, that form does not transmit or store the information you enter — it is not yet connected to an email service or server. No data you type into it leaves your browser or is retained anywhere. If you'd like to get in touch, please email directly using the address provided in the Contact section.

Third-party services

Aside from GitHub Pages (hosting) and Google Fonts (typefaces) described above, this website does not currently integrate any third-party services, embeds, or advertising networks.

Children's privacy

This site is a professional portfolio and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time — for example, if analytics or a working contact form is added. The "Last updated" date above will reflect the most recent revision.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to hello@naomischaefer.com.
